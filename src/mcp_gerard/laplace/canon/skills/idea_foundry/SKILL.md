# idea_foundry

**Status: [EXPERIMENTAL]**

The base of generation is an idea. Before any result, model, or prose, a work needs one root idea that every outward ring serves. This skill forges that seed and keeps it sharp. It is the generating-stage companion to `result_foundry`: idea_foundry seeds the trunk-root, result_foundry forges the results that grow from it.

## What a seeded idea carries
1. **One sentence.** The idea as a single precise thesis in fixed vocabulary - the thing the whole work is about.
2. **The load-bearing claim.** The one assertion the work lives or dies on, named plainly so every result can be checked against it.
3. **Scope, stated positively.** What the work is, said as a commitment rather than a list of exclusions.
4. **Altitude.** The most general, most universal form the idea can bear at this stage; specifics are admitted only as use demands them.

## Principles
- **General first, refine toward use.** Open at maximum generality. Let operationalisation and numerics force the narrowing. Lock scope late, at drafting. Capacious now, slim by drafting.
- **The idea is the trunk-root.** Every outward ring - results, spine, framing, prose - serves this one idea. A ring that serves something else is off-trunk.
- **Revise deliberately.** The idea changes only by a conscious decision, never by drift. A casual phrase becomes a commitment only when written into the seed.
- **Earn the framing.** State the idea in its own terms. Evocative names and inherited framings are adopted once the development produces what they denote.

## Usage
1. State the idea in one sentence at the highest generality it can bear.
2. Name the load-bearing claim - the assertion that, if false, sinks the work.
3. Write the scope as a positive commitment, and the central question the work chases.
4. Record it as the trunk-root (an `idea` ledger) that later rings load and serve.
5. Revisit only deliberately, when a result earns a change to the seed.

*Experimental. Refine whenever seeding a new work reveals a sharper standard for what a root idea must carry.*
