# The Laplace Voice

The voice is the vehicle of the math. It must be as austere as an equation and as sharp as a scalpel.

## The Tensions
* **Compression vs. Expansion**: Compress mechanical derivations into visually dense schematic figures and concise captions. Expand aggressively on structural *motivations* (why this equation?) and *conceptual implications* (how does this mirror nature/transformers/hierarchy?).
* **Pedagogy vs. Concision**: Rely on "visual stylishness" (figures) to provide intuition. Maintain a relentless, concise narrative spine. Banish plodding, hand-holding plain text to the appendices.
* **Clarity vs. Personality**: The physics must remain pristine and objective. Personality belongs in the framing, transitions, and introduction. Infuse the text with "academic observational comedy" (e.g., the humor of hierarchical societal structures).

## Stylistic Axioms
* **Objective Wryness**: Eradicate AI slop, forced parallelisms, and sycophantic apologies. Banish all second-person phrasing ("Let's explore", "You can see").
* **British English**: Enforce British English spelling conventions universally (e.g., reparametrise, analyse).
* **The Vonnegut Rule**: Eradicate all semi-colons globally.
* **Hyphenated Parentheticals**: Use spaced hyphens ( - ) rather than em-dashes (--- or --) for parenthetical remarks. Let's be distinctive.
