## Inertia as compressed structure

**The intuition.** Hierarchical inertia is not an added penalty - it arises naturally from the depth/latency tradeoff of the adaptive normal form. A deep structure is unconditionally stable but frozen (marginal, $\rho\to1^-$): resistance to being moved accumulates with depth. The conjecture: this is saying something fundamental about the nature of *inertia*, and by extension *mass* - inertia as **compressed structure**, mass as accumulated hierarchical depth.

**Provenance.** Surfaced 2026-05-31 during the adaptive-normal-form R5 work (the inertial side), where the latency probe showed deep autonomous loops freeze rather than destabilise.

**Status.** Parked. Explicitly kept out of the adaptive-normal-form manuscript and its backlog - it is a scope risk, not a sense risk. Raise only if the author reopens it or a later project's scope makes it load-bearing.
