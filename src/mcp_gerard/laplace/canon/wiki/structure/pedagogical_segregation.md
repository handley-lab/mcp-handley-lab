# Pedagogical Segregation

* **The Narrative Spine**: The main text of a manuscript is a relentless, high-stakes structural argument. It must not lose momentum.
* **Ruthless Banishment**: Technical and pedagogical derivations, step-by-step proofs, and algebraic hand-holding must be ruthlessly segregated from the narrative spine. Move exhaustive proofs exclusively to the Appendices, leaving behind only the initial assumptions and the final structural bounds.
