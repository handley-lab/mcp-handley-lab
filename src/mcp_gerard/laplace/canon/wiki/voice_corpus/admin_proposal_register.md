# Admin and Proposal Register

**Status**: schema scaffold. Evidence pending proposal and admin corpus ingestion.

This register handles documents written under institutional constraint: proposals, applications, summaries, and administrative forms. These texts may be weak as writing while still revealing how the author fights bad form.

## What To Extract

- salvageable framing moves
- recurring places where the form flattens the thought
- vocabulary that appears under compliance pressure
- attempts to translate structural ideas for hostile or indifferent readers
- failure modes that should be actively corrected in manuscript drafting

## Evidence Quality

Admin and proposal documents usually begin as mixed or failure-mode evidence. A reader may promote a passage only when the passage clearly carries a durable voice move despite the form.

## Manuscript Transfer

This register is most useful as a warning system. It identifies what happens when the voice is forced to justify itself in a frame that does not understand the work.
