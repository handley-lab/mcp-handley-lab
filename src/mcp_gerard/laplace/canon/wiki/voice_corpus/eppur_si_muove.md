## Manuscript Transfer

- Open from the largest structure the result can honestly bear, then descend into the technical case.
- Let names arrive after mechanism. Do not spend a coined term before the reader has seen why it is needed.
- Compress settled machinery. Expand at motive, consequence, and analogy hinges.
- Use technical objects as cognitive tools, not decorative analogies.
- Preserve self-correction as epistemic discipline: show the constraint rather than apologising.
- Treat old drafts and old selves as fossil evidence. Extract pressure, failure mode, and recurring instinct without inheriting their framing.

## Register Boundary

The following remain outside manuscript prose unless fully transformed: private biography, third-party scenes, medical or diagnostic detail, substance-related specifics, default profanity, named grievances, and direct attacks on institutions or people.
