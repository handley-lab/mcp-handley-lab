# Voice Corpus

**Status**: scaffolded on 2026-05-31. Gate 1 calibration and Gate 2 source discovery have been admitted.

This cluster is the deep map beneath [The Laplace Voice](canon://aesthetics/voice_and_style.md) and [The Author](canon://author/the_author.md). Load it only when a task needs high-fidelity knowledge of how Gerard writes or thinks on the page. Ordinary drafting should start from the top-level Voice and Author nodes.

## Purpose

Build an evidence-backed account of one authorial voice projected through several registers. The goal is not to average the corpus. It is to recover the live invariant beneath old work, strong work, failed work, correspondence, personal writing, and manuscripts, then state how each register should carry that invariant without importing freedoms that the medium cannot bear.

## Operating Principles

- **One voice, many projections.** Registers are not separate personalities. They are boundary conditions on one mark.
- **The fossil record is evidence, not law.** Old or failed writing records pressure, drift, and recurring instinct. It does not automatically define the target.
- **Distillation travels.** Raw text, private detail, and long quotations stay in source stores or private working cache. The wiki admits source handles, provenance, distilled claims, and concise evidence pointers.
- **Quality is explicit.** A sample can be anchor, strong, mixed, weak, or failure-mode evidence. The synthesis must say which it is.
- **Tension is managed at the register boundary.** A feature seen in personal writing may explain the voice while still being banned from a manuscript paragraph.
- **No single source is sovereign.** [Eppur Si Muove](canon://voice_corpus/eppur_si_muove.md) is the clearest full-range personal calibration specimen so far. It should reveal mechanics, not rule every register. Manuscripts, correspondence, canon, and admin forms must each earn their own ideal from their own evidence.

## Admission Rule

Every future voice or method claim in this cluster should carry three fields in substance: source class, evidence quality, and register limit. Every factual claim should carry source handle, confidence, temporal status, and privacy tier. A claim without its required fields is a hunch and belongs in working notes, not canon.
