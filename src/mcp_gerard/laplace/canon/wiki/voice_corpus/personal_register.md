# Personal Register

**Status**: schema scaffold. Evidence pending personal corpus ingestion.

The personal register is the freer projection of the voice. It should teach the manuscript register what the voice is trying to do before academic form constrains it.

## Gate 1 Calibration

Source: `EPPUR-PRIMARY`. Status: first personal calibration specimen, high confidence for this source.

The personal register shows the voice at high amplitude. It permits scene, confession, profanity, grief, sacrilege, and bodily detail, but the durable mechanics are structural: pressure-change cadence, spiral motif return, metaphor as cognition, humour as pressure release, and self-observation under stress. This register should teach manuscript prose the motion of thought, not donate its private content.

## Register Limit

Personal writing can explain the full voice, but manuscript drafting should translate rather than copy it. Direct self-address, confessional pressure, and full literary cadence may identify the source of the voice while remaining outside the manuscript surface.
