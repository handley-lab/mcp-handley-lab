# Voice Corpus Register Matrix

**Status**: provisional scaffold. Replace hypotheses with evidence-backed claims after reader passes.

The working hypothesis is one voice projected through several media. Each register is a medium boundary around the same mark, not a separate style.

## Core Mark

The core mark to test across the corpus. Gate 1 proposes these as provisional calibration claims from [Eppur Si Muove](canon://voice_corpus/eppur_si_muove.md), not cross-register law:

- structural thinking before surface polish
- declarative movement from frame to consequence
- live metaphor used as machinery
- compression around derivation and expansion around motive
- impatience with inherited forms that waste attention
- humour as diagnosis of hierarchy and pretence
- explicit handling of recursion, method, and self-reference

## Gate 1 One-Voice Hypothesis

The first calibration source suggests that the invariant is not a tone but a movement: choose a large frame, force particulars to pay rent inside it, catch the self or system evading the frame, then return to the concrete object with the abstraction sharpened. This is a candidate invariant, not a settled one. Each later gate must test whether the movement survives under that register's admissible evidence.

## Evidence Weighting

- **Calibration specimen.** A source that shows the voice at high amplitude in one register. It proposes mechanics.
- **Register anchor.** A source that defines a target for one register after same-register comparison. It constrains mechanics.
- **Cross-register invariant.** A claim that survives several source classes and earns compact top-level use.
- **Failure-mode evidence.** A source that shows what to avoid or how a form distorts the voice.

`EPPUR-PRIMARY` is currently a calibration specimen for personal writing. It is not a manuscript anchor, correspondence anchor, canon anchor, or universal ideal.

## Register Boundaries

- **Manuscript.** Permits objective claim, structural motivation, and dry conceptual framing. Restrains direct address, confessional force, and loose metaphor. Main risk: over-sanitising until the thought loses its author.
- **Personal.** Permits full metaphor, emotional torque, narrative pressure, and direct self-reckoning. Restrains technical over-control where vulnerability is doing work. Main risk: mistaking an old self-state for the durable voice.
- **Correspondence.** Permits direct command, peer address, compressed judgement, and local warmth. Restrains full literary architecture and long conceptual digressions. Main risk: turning tactical mail into global doctrine.
- **Admin and proposal.** Permits strategic framing under hostile form constraints. Restrains treating institutional compliance as the target voice. Main risk: learning submission posture instead of voice.
- **Canon and collaboration.** Permits imperative method, meta-level naming, and compact rules. Restrains overfitting to one session mood. Main risk: letting local lore harden into universal protocol.

## Cross-Register Questions

- Which sentence shapes recur when the work is good?
- Where does metaphor carry structure rather than decoration?
- How does the page move from irritation to insight?
- What does the author expand when the writing is alive?
- What does the author compress when the writing trusts the reader?
- Which forms flatten the voice, and what compensating moves restore it?

## Synthesis Target

The mature cluster should produce:

- a concise invariant for the voice
- a register-specific ideal schema
- a list of transferable moves
- a list of register-bounded moves
- a failure-mode ledger
- drafting instructions for manuscript prose that preserve the author without breaking the medium
